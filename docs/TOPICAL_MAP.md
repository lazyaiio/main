# Topical map for the LazyAI blog (draft, no search-volume data)

**Audience:** owners and managers of small-business Facebook Pages. **Goal of the blog:** earn trust and organic visits for people who need a content plan, better posts, and scheduling. **Language:** English (American).
**Method:** three silos, one **pillar** guide each (broad topic, 1,700+ words), and **supporting** articles (one narrow question each, 1,250+ words) that link up to the pillar and sideways to siblings. Every article ends with one next step and links to a free tool where it fits. The target queries below are hypotheses until checked in Search Console and a keyword tool.

## Internal linking rules used
1. Every supporting article links to its pillar. 2. Every pillar links to its supporting articles and to the other pillars. 3. Each article has at least three links to other articles, tools, or pricing. 4. Anchor text describes the target ("how often to post on Facebook"), not "click here". 5. No orphan articles (the checklist counts links; the article page also adds related articles automatically).

## Other ways to get organic visits (honest notes)
Free tools (started here), comparison and alternative pages, business-type pages, listings on product directories, answering questions in relevant communities, a Facebook Page that uses the product daily, and page speed. All of these take weeks to months; none is a guarantee.
