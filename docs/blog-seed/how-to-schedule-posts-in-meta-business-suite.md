Meta Business Suite is the free tool that Meta provides for managing Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts. It has a built-in way to write a post now and publish it later. If you run a Page and you do not yet use a scheduling app, it is the first tool to try. This guide shows how to schedule posts in Meta Business Suite step by step, how to find, change, and delete them afterward, and what to do when something goes wrong.

One warning comes first. Meta changes its screens and button names from time to time, so what you see may differ from the steps below. The overall flow has stayed similar for a long time, and you can search Meta's Business Help Center for "schedule a post" to see the current wording. For the bigger picture of scheduling options, see our guide on [how to schedule Facebook Page posts](/blog/how-to-schedule-facebook-page-posts).

## What you need before you start

- **A Facebook Page.** Business Suite schedules posts for Pages, not for personal profiles.
- **The right access.** You need a role on the Page that allows you to publish, such as full control or content permission. Task access, which lets you work from business tools, can also work. Ask a Page admin if you are unsure.
- **Your content ready.** The text, the photo or video, and any link.
- **A time zone check.** Make sure your account shows the time zone where you want to schedule.

Facebook Groups follow different rules, and Business Suite may not cover them in the same way. This guide is about Pages.

## Step 1: Open Meta Business Suite

There are two common ways in.

1. Go to your Facebook Page and look for Meta Business Suite in the left menu.
2. Or go to business.facebook.com and sign in.

Then check the Page selector at the top left, and make sure that the right Page is chosen. If you manage several Pages, scheduling for the wrong one is an easy mistake.

## Step 2: Start a new post

From the home screen, click the button to create a post. You can also open the Planner in the left menu, which shows a calendar. Hover over the date you want and choose to schedule a post, and the date is filled in for you.

A window opens with a place for your text and media, and a preview of how the post will look.

[[image: person creating a post on a laptop in a bright office | Creating a post on a laptop]]

## Step 3: Write the post and add media

Type your text in the box. Add a photo or video by choosing the upload option. Pay attention to:

- **The first line.** It should make sense on its own. Our guide on [hooks and calls to action](/blog/facebook-post-hooks-and-calls-to-action) can help.
- **The photo.** A clear, well-lit image works best. Check the preview to see how it is cropped.
- **The link.** If you add one, open it on a phone to make sure it works.
- **Alt text.** If you see a field to describe the image, fill it in. Our guide on [alt text for social media images](/blog/alt-text-for-social-media-images) explains how.

Choose where the post goes. If your Page is linked to Instagram, you may see a choice to post to both. Make sure only the places you want are selected.

## Step 4: Choose the scheduling option

Look for the scheduling options below the post editor. The wording varies, but you will see something like a toggle to set a date and time, or an arrow next to the publish button with a schedule choice.

1. Switch on the option to set a date and time.
2. Choose the date from the calendar.
3. Choose the time.
4. Check the time zone label if one is shown.

Meta sets rules for how soon and how far ahead you can schedule, such as a minimum lead time. The numbers have been reported differently in different places and may change, so rely on the message that Business Suite shows you, not on a figure from an article.

If Business Suite shows suggested times, based on when your audience is active, treat them as hints. Our guide to the [best time to post on Facebook](/blog/best-time-to-post-on-facebook) explains how to test your own times.

## Step 5: Confirm

Click the schedule button. The post should appear in your Planner, on the date and at the time you chose, and in the list of scheduled content. If it does not, go back and check that you chose scheduling, not publish.

## How to find your scheduled posts

- **Planner:** a calendar view of what is published and what is scheduled.
- **Content or Posts section:** a list with a tab for scheduled items.

Use the filters, if shown, to see only posts, or only the Page you want.

## How to edit, reschedule, or delete a scheduled post

1. Open the Planner or the scheduled list.
2. Click the post.
3. Choose to edit, reschedule, or delete.
4. Save your changes.

Do this at least a few days ahead if you can. If you change the media, check the preview again.

## A weekly routine that works

Scheduling ahead works best with a short check.

- **Monday, five minutes:** open the Planner and look at the week.
- **Check each post.** Is the text correct, the link working, and the time right?
- **Check the Page access.** Make sure at least two people can manage the Page.
- **Reply to comments.** Scheduled posts still need answers.

If you want to plan a whole month at once, our guide on [scheduling a month of Facebook posts in one hour](/blog/how-to-schedule-a-month-of-facebook-posts-in-one-hour) shows a routine.

## When scheduling does not work

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| You cannot see the schedule option | Check your role on the Page. You may not have permission |
| The post did not publish | Look for an error message, and check your Page access and media |
| The time is wrong | Check the time zone in your account and in the post |
| The Page is missing | Check you are signed in to the right account |
| An image failed to upload | Try a different file, a standard JPG or PNG, or a smaller size |

Our guide on [why a scheduled Facebook post failed](/blog/why-a-scheduled-facebook-post-failed) covers these causes in more depth.

## Working with a team

If more than one person handles the Page, agree on who writes, who checks, and who schedules. A simple rule helps: the person who schedules a post is not the only person who has read it. Keep the weekly check on a fixed day, and note any changes, such as a sale that ended early, in one shared place. Make sure at least two trusted people have the access they need, so that a holiday or a role change does not stop your posts.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Scheduling for the wrong Page.** Check the selector.
- **Scheduling too many posts the same day.** Spread them out.
- **Forgetting the time zone.** Confirm it first.
- **Not checking the preview.** Photos may crop in ways you do not expect.
- **Leaving no gaps.** Keep room for news.
- **Never reviewing.** A scheduled post can become wrong, such as a sale that ended.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Meta Business Suite free?

It is offered free by Meta for managing Pages and linked accounts. Boosting a post or running ads costs money, but scheduling a regular post does not.

### Can I schedule from my phone?

Meta has a mobile app for Business Suite that lets you schedule posts. The screens differ from desktop.

### Can I schedule a personal profile post?

Business Suite is for Pages. Profiles have different options, and some are limited. Check Meta's help pages for the current rules.

### How far ahead can I schedule?

Meta sets a window, and the numbers may change. The editor will tell you if your date is outside it.
